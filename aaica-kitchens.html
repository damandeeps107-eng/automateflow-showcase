09 / Common Questions Transparent Answers
How does AAICA save 35–40% compared to retail showrooms? +

Retail showrooms in Delhi NCR pay heavy showroom rents, sales commissions, and middleman dealer markups of 35% to 45%. Because we are an in-house factory atelier with automated German CNC machinery in Jagat Puri, you buy directly from the manufacturer at zero retail markup.

How do you guarantee termite-proof & 100% boiling-waterproof kitchens? +

We exclusively build wet zone base cabinets using genuine IS:710 Marine Grade Boiling Waterproof (BWP) Plywood and calibrated anti-termite HDHMR boards. All edges are sealed with 2mm PVC edge banding using PUR waterproof glue on automated machines, ensuring moisture never penetrates.

Are 3D design visualizations and site laser measurements really free? +

Yes, 100% free with zero obligation. Our senior designer visits your flat or villa across Delhi, Gurgaon, or Noida with digital laser measurement tools and physical shutter finish swatches to prepare your custom photorealistic 3D layout.

What hardware brands do you use, and what is the warranty? +

We use certified German hardware partners: Hettich, Hafele, and Blum soft-close hinges, tandem boxes, and lift-up systems. All our kitchens come backed by an official 10-Year Written Replacement Warranty certificate on cabinetry and hardware.

What is the delivery and installation timeline? +

Because cabinetry modules are 100% pre-fabricated, edge-banded, and quality-tested in our factory, on-site installation at your home takes only 48 to 72 hours with clean, modular assembly — zero sawdust, zero noise, and zero delay.
