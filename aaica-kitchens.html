Conventional Showrooms & Online Portals

What typically happens behind expensive mall facades.

1. Core Material
Commercial particle board or MDF prone to moisture swelling and screw degradation. Marine ply carries a 30%–45% exorbitant surcharge.
2. Motion Hardware
Basic or white-label proprietary OEM fittings without manufacturer lifetime warranties; branded European hinges cost substantially extra.
3. Manufacturing Transparency
Production is outsourced to distant 3rd-party contract hubs. Clients are strictly not permitted to inspect raw materials prior to delivery.
4. Markup Structure
Up to 40% of the invoice covers prime mall rentals, corporate overheads, celebrity marketing, and dealer sales commissions.
5. Execution & Handover
Frequent 60 to 90-day supply chain delays; execution delegated to rotating local carpenters with ticket-based support.

Everything You Need To Know

Direct clarity on materials, factory fabrication, hardware warranties, and timelines.

Indian kitchens undergo heavy steam, turmeric, water exposure, and spice grinding. Commercial MDF and particle board absorb ambient humidity and swell within 2–3 years, degrading screw holding. AAICA uses exclusively calibrated 18mm IS:710 Marine Grade BWP ply with boiling waterproof bonding and an unconditional 10-year replacement warranty against water delamination, termite, or borer attack.
Yes, absolutely. Unlike mall studios that outsource to third-party contract units, AAICA operates our own direct joinery atelier at F-165 Jagat Puri, Near F Block Bus Stand, Delhi-110051. Homeowners are actively encouraged to visit, inspect their raw marine ply sheets, verify German PUR edge-banding, and check certified Blum/Häfele hardware before panel sizing commences.
By purchasing directly from our manufacturing atelier, homeowners save 30% to 40% on identical specifications. A standard 22 Rft kitchen in 18mm IS:710 Marine Ply with Senosan acrylic and Blum hardware costs between ₹2.3L to ₹2.75L at AAICA, whereas retail mall studios quote ₹3.7L to ₹4.4L for comparable grades due to 35%+ showroom markups and sales commissions.
We use 100% authentic Austrian Blum Tandembox/Legrabox drawers and German Häfele soft-close hinges backed by the manufacturer's lifetime replacement warranty (certified for 200,000 opening cycles). Every fitting carries official serial authentication.
From finalized 3D CAD blueprints, precision CNC fabrication at our Jagat Puri atelier takes approximately 25 to 30 days. On-site installation is completed in 48 hours using pre-drilled dowel-and-minifix joinery with zero cutting dust inside your home.
