Wall panel installation steps

  1. Measure walls and select sheets

    We inspect wall dampness, measure exact height and width, and match panel shades to your room lighting. You can compare walnut, charcoal, and marble textures before cutting begins.

  2. Cut and lock panels dry

    Profiles are trimmed on site and fixed onto battens or directly onto sound plaster without wet cement or dust. Interlocking joints align plumb from floor skirting to ceiling.

  3. Seal panel trims and light cutouts

    Corner beads, L-profiles, switch plates, and ceiling light openings are sealed flush with silicone before handover. The room is ready to use the same day.

Common questions. Damp resistance, installation time, and pricing.

Do PVC panels hold up on damp walls?
Yes. PVC and WPC panels are waterproof and termite-resistant, and can be mounted on battens so trapped wall moisture can breathe.
How long does one room take?
A standard bedroom accent wall finishes in one day; full four-wall and ceiling coverage takes 2 to 4 days.
Can I buy sheets without installation?
Yes. We supply full sheets, louvers, and matching edge trims if your own carpenter is fitting them.
How is the final cost calculated?
Pricing depends on wall square footage, panel thickness (8 mm PVC vs 24 mm WPC), and corner trim count.
Do you handle electrical and switch cutouts?
Yes. Switch plates, fan hooks, and recessed LED ceiling cutouts are trimmed cleanly during fitting.
How do you clean PVC and UV marble sheets?
Wipe surfaces with a damp microfiber cloth and mild soap; avoid harsh abrasives on high-gloss UV marble sheets.
